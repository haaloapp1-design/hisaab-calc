# Privacy Policy — Hisab

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

Hisab ("the app") is a calculator, unit converter, currency converter and set of everyday
calculation tools for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Summary

**Hisab does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information.**
There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following **only on your own phone**, so it is there next time you open the app:

- your calculation history and memory slots,
- your settings (theme, accent colour, decimal places, angle unit, vibration and sound, history length),
- your converter favourites and chosen currencies,
- the last downloaded table of exchange rates.

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time in
**Settings → Clear all saved data**, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## Internet use

The app connects to the internet for one purpose: downloading a public table of currency exchange rates
from ExchangeRate-API (`https://open.er-api.com`). The request is made over an encrypted (HTTPS)
connection and contains no personal information — only a request for the latest rates. As with any
web request, the rate provider's servers can see your device's IP address; their handling of it is
governed by their own privacy policy (https://www.exchangerate-api.com/terms).

## Permissions

- **Internet** — to download exchange rates (see above). This is the app's only permission.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Children

The app is a general-audience calculator. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone,
including children under 13.
