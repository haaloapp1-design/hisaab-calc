## Full description  (max 4000)

```
Hisab (حساب) is a clean, fast calculator for everyday sums and serious maths — with a unit converter, live currency rates and ten ready-made tools, all in one app. No ads, no sign-in, no tracking.

ACCURATE BY DESIGN
Hisab uses exact decimal arithmetic, so 0.1 + 0.2 shows 0.3 — not 0.30000000000000004. Results update live as you type, and errors are explained in plain words ("Can't divide by zero").

THREE CALCULATORS
• Basic — big keys, percent that works the way you expect (12,500 + 18% = 14,750), repeat-equals, thousands separators.
• Scientific — sin, cos, tan and their inverses, powers and roots, ln, log, eˣ, 10ˣ, factorial, π, e, brackets, Ans, degrees or radians, and implicit multiplication like 2π or 3(4+1).
• Programmer — HEX, DEC, OCT and BIN side by side, AND, OR, XOR, NOT, NAND, shifts and mod, with 8, 16, 32 or 64-bit word sizes.

UNIT CONVERTER
12 categories: length, area, volume, mass, temperature, speed, time, data, pressure, energy, power and angle — including local units like marla, kanal, tola and maund. Search any unit, swap with one tap, see common equivalents at a glance and star your favourite pairs.

CURRENCY CONVERTER
Live exchange rates for about 60 major world currencies, refreshed automatically and saved on your phone, so conversions keep working offline with the last rates.

10 EVERYDAY TOOLS
• Loan / EMI — monthly instalment, total interest and a full monthly schedule
• Percentage — X% of Y, percentage change, ratios
• Discount and tax — sale price with GST
• Tip and split — share a bill fairly
• Age — exact age in years, months and days, and days to your next birthday
• Date difference — days between dates, or add and subtract days
• BMI — metric or imperial, with the WHO category
• GPA / grades — weighted average on 4.0, 5.0 or 10-point scales
• Equation solver — linear and quadratic, including complex roots
• Statistics — mean, median, mode, range and standard deviation

HISTORY AND MEMORY
Every calculation is saved by day. Search it, tap to reuse, copy or delete. Ten memory slots with M+, M− and recall.

MAKE IT YOURS
Light, dark or system theme, four accent colours, decimal places, angle unit, key vibration and sounds.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Everything stays on your device. Hisab has no account, no ads and no analytics. The only thing it downloads is the public exchange-rate table.
```
